# Proposed catalogue fields

Stable ID; approved title and public description; media type; date/date range and certainty; creator/contributor; community and approved place names; language and orthography; repository and accession number; source URL; copyright/license/permission evidence; consent status and permitted uses; restrictions; cultural review status/reviewer/date; board approval status/date/decision reference; access level (private, designated members, board, public); separate original and public-derivative storage keys; transcript/captions/keywords; created/updated timestamps and change log.

**Release rule:** public visibility requires rights confirmation, recorded consent and permitted uses, cultural review where applicable, and board approval according to TACS policy. Missing information must never default to public.
